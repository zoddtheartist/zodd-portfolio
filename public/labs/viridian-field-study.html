two hummingbirds in Colorado, a colour study

VIRIDIAN

the study

I watched these birds for days. I came home with a few hundred photographs and kept two.

It started as a colour study. The green on her back and the red on his throat aren’t pigment — there is no red in that feather. It is structure, and it changes depending on where you happen to be standing when he turns. That is the thing I was trying to photograph, and it is why the palettes below are pulled out of the photographs rather than picked by me.

The flight numbers further down are not mine. They are cited.
